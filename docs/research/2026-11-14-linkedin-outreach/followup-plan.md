# Follow-up plan (2026-11-14)

What to send after the invite, when, and when to stop. The texts are in
[message-templates.md](message-templates.md). Nothing here is sent by Claude: you send every
message yourself.

## The short version

- **The step that was missing is the message after they accept.** In week one, 8 of the 10 people
  who accepted never heard from you again, and both replies came from the other person starting
  the conversation.
- **The app can't tell whether it has happened since.** Your Nov 7 plan scheduled follow-ups for
  7 of those 8 on Nov 10-12 and 1 on Nov 17, but the app has no box for "follow-up sent". On
  Nov 14 the same 8 still showed no reply and nothing recorded. So check before you send anything.
- **The sequence:** invite on the day you apply → one follow-up after they accept → at most one
  last nudge (needs your OK) → stop. At most three messages per person.
- **Timing:** 08:30-10:00 their time, Tuesday to Thursday (your rule). Two changes are proposed,
  each needing your OK: Monday to Wednesday in Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain, whose week runs Sunday
  to Thursday; and the follow-up the next working morning after the accept instead of 4-6 working
  days later.
- **Withdraw invites nobody accepted after 3 weeks.** In sales data, 91% of the invites that are
  ever accepted are accepted within 2 weeks, and LinkedIn can restrict accounts with many pending
  invites.

## The sequence for one job

| step | when | what | stop if |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Invite | the same sitting as the application | the invite note (A or B, from the job's id, if you OK test T1) | the job closes |
| 2. Follow-up | after they accept: 4-6 working days today; the next working morning if you OK the change | one closed question + the location line + an easy way out | they reply, or the job closes |
| 3. Last nudge (needs your OK) | 5-7 working days after the follow-up, no answer | "One last note on the [Role] role: is it still open? ..." | always: this is the last message |
| 4. After a meaningful reply | as soon as you can | answer it; if the role is still open, the "flag my application" ask (needs your OK; a different version for the hiring manager) | they say no, or it's done |
| No accept after 3 weeks | weekly | withdraw the invite (you, by hand) | |

For a German job where you asked before applying (needs your OK), the ask was message 1. After a
"yes", apply, then send the reply in
[message-templates.md](message-templates.md#6-short-answers-to-the-usual-replies); that's message
2, and the last nudge, if any, is message 3.

**Hard stops:**

- At most three messages per person. Silence after that means no, and you leave it there.
- Never write about a job the app shows as closed. The Nov 7 batch dropped one follow-up for
  exactly this reason.
- A clear "no" ("we don't sponsor", "local candidates only") gets one short thank-you, then the
  card is closed with the quote.

## When to send

### The day and hour

08:30-10:00 **their** time, on these days:

| where | days | why |
|---|---|---|
| UAE, Europe, the UK | Tuesday to Thursday (your rule today) | their week runs Monday to Friday |
| Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain | Tuesday to Thursday today; Monday to Wednesday if you OK the change | their week runs Sunday to Thursday, so Thursday is their Friday and Sunday their Monday |

- Never the first morning of their week, the last afternoon of it, or the weekend.
- The day matters less than sending at all. The day-of-week effects in the data are small
  (Belkins, sales: Thursday and Monday best at just above 7.4% replies, Friday and Saturday 6.9%;
  LinkedIn's recruiter data: Saturday 8% below average, Friday 4% below).

**Beirut times for 09:00 their time** (Beirut is UTC+2 from late October to late March, UTC+3
the rest of the year):

| their country | their time zone | 09:00 there = Beirut, winter | Beirut, summer |
|---|---|---|---|
| UAE | UTC+4 | 07:00 | 08:00 |
| Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain | UTC+3 | 08:00 | 09:00 |
| Finland | UTC+2 (summer +3) | 09:00 | 09:00 |
| Netherlands, Germany, Spain, Italy | UTC+1 (summer +2) | 10:00 | 10:00 |
| UK, Ireland | UTC+0 (summer +1) | 11:00 | 11:00 |

### How long after the accept

- **Today's rule:** 4-6 working days after the accept, once.
- **What the data says:** nothing about job seekers. In sales data the median accept comes after 1
  day, 62% of accepts come within 24 hours, and 60% of replies come within the first week (Belkins,
  152,636 contacts, 2025-26). Belkins and Expandi both advise messaging within a few days of the
  accept (advice, not a measured comparison).
- **The case for waiting:** both week-one replies came from the other person writing first.
  Waiting gives them room to do that.
- **The case for not waiting:** the note is freshest right after they accept, and the longer the
  gap, the easier it is to forget to send (the 8 waiting people show that).
- **Recommendation (needs your OK): send it the next working morning after the accept**, in their
  08:30-10:00 window, on any of their working days except the first morning of the week. If they
  write first in the meantime, answer that instead. This is a judgment call, not a finding: the
  evidence leans this way, and the practical gain (nothing waits long enough to be forgotten) is
  certain. It needs one habit: **a 2-minute look at LinkedIn's notifications each working
  morning**, since the weekly review only sees accepts once a week.
- **If you want a check that it doesn't do worse: test T2**
  ([outreach-experiments.md](outreach-experiments.md)). Half the jobs (by id) keep today's 4-6
  days, half get the next working morning, and both are counted per accept, so neither version
  loses the people who write first. A tie goes to the next-morning version.

## Invites that are never accepted

- **Withdraw after 3 weeks.** In Belkins' sales data, 91% of the invites that are ever accepted are
  accepted within 2 weeks. LinkedIn can restrict an account when "many of your invitations have
  been ignored, left pending, or marked as spam", and you had 128 pending on Nov 7.
- Withdrawing is silent: LinkedIn doesn't notify the person. You can't invite the same person
  again for up to 3 weeks, and you shouldn't anyway.
- Do it once a week: My Network → Manage → Sent → withdraw anything older than 3 weeks.
- **Optional, needs your OK:** for a grade 4-5 job, one InMail to the same person (Premium Career
  gives 5 a month, and a credit comes back if they answer within 90 days). It's the same person
  through another channel, so "one person per job" still holds, but it's a new step. Use the
  follow-up's wording.

## After someone replies

- **Answer the same day if you can**, during their working hours.
- **Meaningful reply, role still open:** the "flag my application" ask (needs your OK), with the
  paste-ready block and the CV you applied with. Use the hiring-manager version for a manager;
  skip it for the recruiter (they already have your application).
- **They ask for the CV:** send the same PDF the application used.
- **They say no:** one line of thanks, then close the card with their words as the reason.
- **They mention an interview or a referral:** add the tag (`#interview MM-DD via-outreach`,
  `#referral MM-DD`) so the weekly review counts it.
