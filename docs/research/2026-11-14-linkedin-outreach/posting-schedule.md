# LinkedIn posting: when to post and what to try (2026-11-16)

Notes for posting on LinkedIn (your own posts, not invites or messages). Saved as given;
not checked against your own numbers yet.

## 1. Best times to post in 2026

Buffer analyzed **4.8 million LinkedIn posts** and found that Wednesday at 4 PM was the
strongest overall slot, with late afternoons generally doing better than mornings. Sprout
Social's research instead favors Tuesday through Thursday during working hours.
(Sources: buffer.com, Sprout Social)

For the target audience (European tech professionals, recruiters and hiring managers), a
schedule to test:

| Day | Time (Beirut) | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday | 2 PM | Industry question or debate |
| Wednesday | 5 PM | Strongest technical or opinion post |
| Thursday | 5 PM | Personal engineering story |
| Friday | 4 PM | Funny or relatable developer post |

These are slots to test, not proven best times for your own audience. Wednesday at 5 PM
Beirut is 4 PM in Germany in November.

## 2. LinkedIn growth ideas worth trying

1. **Hook people in the first two lines.** The opening decides whether someone expands the
   post. Use a surprising claim, a specific result, or an interesting failure.
2. **Ask a question developers genuinely disagree about.** For example, "Should developers
   still review every line of AI-generated code?" Specific questions tend to get more
   discussion than generic calls to comment. Metricool's 2026 study found that posts with
   questions got substantially more comments. (Source: Metricool)
3. **Test PDF carousels and multi-image posts.** They work well for tutorials, lessons and
   checklists. Metricool found that carousels and multi-image posts often do better than
   ordinary single-image and video posts. (Source: Metricool)
